Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 18, 2026 • iOS Application

1. Our Commitment to Privacy

AURA – The Sovereign Registry ("AURA", "we", "our", or "us") was engineered from the ground up on the principle of digital discretion and security. We believe that membership in an exclusive registry must be accompanied by absolute respect for personal data.

Zero Data Monetization: We do NOT sell, rent, lease, or monetize your personal information to third parties, data brokers, or advertising networks under any circumstances.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum data required to authenticate your status and display your membership seat on the Global Registry.

A. Profile Data Provided Voluntarily

  • Display Name / Alias: The chosen name under which your registry seat is listed.
  • Nation of Registry: Your declared country or territory of affiliation.
  • Sovereign Motto: An optional personal statement shown on your 3D Black Diamond Pass.
  • Insignia / Avatar: The luxury emblem selected for your profile card.

B. In-App Purchase & Transaction Validation

All financial processing is handled strictly and directly by Apple StoreKit 2.

  • We do not collect or store credit card numbers, billing addresses, or financial credentials.
  • Apple transmits an anonymized, cryptographically verified JSON Web Signature (JWS) receipt token. This token confirms your payment and guarantees your immutable sequence number (e.g. #001, #015).

C. Push Notification Tokens

  • If you permit push notifications, our system registers an anonymous device token through Apple Push Notification service (APNs) and Firebase Cloud Messaging to deliver alerts when new sovereign members enter the registry.
  • You may revoke notification permissions at any time via iOS Settings.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • Public Registry Display: Displaying your verified rank, country badge, and motto within the global high-society index.
  • Pass Minting: Generating your interactive 3D gyroscopic Member Pass with cryptographic proof of authenticity.
  • Notification Routing: Dispatching real-time registry alerts.
  • Diagnostics: Analyzing anonymous crash logs to ensure high application uptime and performance.

4. Third-Party Infrastructure

We work exclusively with enterprise-grade technology partners:

  • Apple Inc.: Processes in-app purchases, receipt validation, and APNs delivery under Apple's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Cloud / Firebase: Encrypted cloud database and messaging infrastructure under Google's Privacy Policy.

5. Data Security & Encryption

All data in transit is protected using modern TLS 1.3 encryption. Registry records stored on Firebase Firestore are protected with 256-bit AES encryption at rest. Strict database security rules prevent unauthorized access or tampering.

6. Account & Data Deletion Rights

In accordance with Apple App Store Review Guidelines and international privacy standards (including GDPR and CCPA), you have the right to request deletion of your data at any time.

How to request deletion: Email us at support@aura-registry.com with your verified Member ID or transaction hash, or submit a request on our GitHub Repository. All personal identifiers will be permanently removed from active databases within 30 days.
